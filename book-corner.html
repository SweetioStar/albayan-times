Book/Movie Suggestions

Books and movies our students recommend — picked by them, for you.

Featured Suggestion

Harry Potter

by J.K. Rowling

Book

Harry Potter is an 11-year-old orphan who lives with his mean aunt, uncle, and cousin. On his birthday, he discovers a big secret: he is a famous wizard! Harry goes to Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry to learn magic. There, he makes two best friends, Ron and Hermione, and learns how to fly on a broomstick. The story gets exciting when Harry and his friends find out that someone is trying to steal the Sorcerer's Stone.

Suggested by Hessa Ahmad Almatawaa | Grade 5B
