Discussion Guidelines

To keep our conversations respectful and productive for everyone, please follow these simple rules.

1

Be Respectful

Treat everyone with kindness. Disagree politely and never attack a person — only discuss ideas.

2

Stay On Topic

Keep your posts related to the discussion. If you have a new idea, start a fresh post with a clear title.

3

Share Creatively

Videos, pictures, quotes, and interesting facts are welcome. The more creative, the better.

4

Think Before Posting

Everything you write is public. Re-read your post before publishing and make sure it represents you well.
